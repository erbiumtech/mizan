## What this is

Everything assigned to you, across every project you can see, in one place. Nobody sets this up —
it is built from your name being on work items.

## The columns

**To do**, **In progress** and **Done**, which are the three status *categories* rather than the
statuses themselves. That is deliberate: your work may come from several projects, and those
projects need not use the same workflow. A project that calls its middle column "In QA" and one
that calls it "With the client" both appear here under In progress.

## Moving a card

The same as on a project board, and with the same rules — a column limit or a workflow rule can
refuse a move, and the card goes back with a message saying why. Dropping a card in **Done** here
resolves it in its own project.

## If it is empty

Then nothing is assigned to you. Items you reported or are watching do not appear here; this screen
answers "what am I supposed to be doing", not "what have I touched". Use the Work Items list with
the search for those.

## Roles and permissions

Everybody. There is nothing on this screen that is not already yours.
