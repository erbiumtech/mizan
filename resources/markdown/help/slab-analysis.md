## What this is

The FBR salaried schedule for the fiscal year, with how many of your employees fall in
each band and what is being withheld from each.

*Tax Summary* answers the same question per person: what each employee was withheld
and the slab they fell in. This one answers it per slab, which is the shape a
budgeting conversation needs.

## Which band somebody is in

**The projected annual income decides it, not the month.** A slab is a fact about
somebody's year — it is what the calculator uses — and placing people by one month's
earnings would move them between bands every time a bonus was paid.

The figures come from the annual tax projection, which every payslip rewrites as it is
raised or changed. So the report moves with payroll rather than being a separate
calculation that can disagree with it.

## "Below the first threshold"

The last row, when there is one: everybody whose projected income does not reach the
first band. They are not an error and not missing data — they are the headcount paying
no income tax at all, which is a number worth knowing.

Somebody with no projection at all is somebody nobody has run payroll for this year.
They are counted in the employee tile and in no band.

## When it is empty

No slabs are seeded for the fiscal year. They are the FBR schedule and change with
every Finance Act, so a new year needs them entered under Salary Slabs before any of
this — or any payroll tax at all — can be computed.

## Roles and permissions

`ReportView` and the Payroll module.
