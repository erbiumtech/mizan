## What this is

What happens next. **The one screen here that tells you to do something** rather than
recording what you did.

Everything else in the CRM is a register of the past. This is a list to work.

## Why it matters more than it looks <!-- requires: OpportunityView -->

An open deal with **no next action** is the clearest warning sign a pipeline gives. It is a
deal somebody is thinking about and has not decided a step for — and thinking about a deal is
not working it.

Deals in that state show in red on the Deals list, and appear in the rotting report.

## A date is enough <!-- requires: OpportunityUpdate -->

Most next actions are "call them Tuesday". Forcing a time on that just makes somebody invent
one.

## Snoozing keeps the original date <!-- requires: OpportunityUpdate -->

**Deliberately.** Pushing an action out does not rewrite when it was first due, so *how many
times something has been pushed* stays visible — which is usually the more useful fact than
the new date. An action snoozed four times is telling you something the fifth date will not.

A snoozed action is not "due" until its snooze date arrives, so it leaves your working list
without disappearing from the record.

## Roles and permissions

Shares the Deals permissions: **View** `OpportunityView`, **create and complete**
`OpportunityUpdate`. Logging a call and planning the next step are the same job as working the
deal, so they are not granted separately.
