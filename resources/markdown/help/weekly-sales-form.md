## What this is

Five numbers and a sentence about your week.

**Only for a company without the CRM pipeline.** One with it already has every figure
on this form as a consequence of working the week, and the Weekly Sales report reads
them. This is for a company that has a sales team and no CRM, where the alternative is
no report at all.

## Why it is short <!-- requires: OpportunityView -->

Five fields and it stays five. A form with twenty is a form people stop filling in,
and a weekly report nobody fills in is worse than none — the gaps read as nothing
having happened.

**Anything worth saying** is one or two sentences: what is stuck, what is about to
land. It is the field a manager actually reads.

## The week

Enter any day in the week; it is filed under that week's Monday. One form per person
per week — sending a second corrects the first rather than adding to it.

## Roles and permissions

Everybody can send their own and see their own. `OpportunityView` shows everybody's,
and `OpportunityUpdate` corrects somebody else's — a figure typed wrong is corrected,
not argued with.
