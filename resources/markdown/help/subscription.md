## What this is

The company's plan, its seats, its trial or renewal date, and the invoices
raised for it. Seats are counted from active employees on the first of each
month.

## Changing plan

Choose the plan and confirm. A change takes effect at once; the next invoice
is prorated from the day of the change. Downgrading below the modules in use
is refused until those modules are switched off.

## Export

**Export my data** builds a complete copy of the company's database and files
and e-mails a download link. It is also what runs before a company is closed.
