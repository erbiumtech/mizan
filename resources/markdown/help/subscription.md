## What this is

The company's plan, its seats, its trial or renewal date, and the invoices
raised for it. Seats are counted from active employees on the first of each
month.

## Changing plan

Choose the plan and confirm. The new plan's modules are switched on at once.

**Nothing is switched off.** A module you already have stays, even when the plan
you move to does not include it — a downgrade that silently took away the screen
somebody was using would be the wrong thing to do without asking.

**The price on your agreement does not change by itself.** It was agreed with a
person and the monthly meter has never written it. Tell us you have changed plan
and we will reissue the agreement.

## Seats, and what you are billed for

Your bill counts **the greatest of three numbers**: your active employees, the
seats you hold, and the plan minimum. Active employees are counted on the 1st of
each month.

**Hold seats** is for a month you are hiring into. Holding twenty seats means you
are billed for at least twenty whether or not the people have started — and if
thirty start, you are billed for thirty. It raises the floor and never the
ceiling, so it cannot be used to cap what you owe.

It applies on the next meter, on the 1st. Set it back to zero to stop holding any.

## Export and backups

**Export my data** builds a complete copy of the company's database and files
and e-mails a download link. It is also what runs before a company is closed.

**Back up now** takes a database snapshot immediately, beside the nightly ones —
worth doing before a bulk import or a payroll run you are nervous about. The
snapshots are listed on the page and are not downloadable from it: they are
encrypted with a key held by whoever runs the installation, and restoring one is
their job. For a copy you keep yourself, use the export.

## Your domain

On a plan that includes it, the panel can answer on your own host, say
`hr.yourcompany.pk`. Create a CNAME record pointing that host at the address
shown on the page, wait for it to propagate, and save it under **Custom
domain**. The domain is checked before it is accepted, so a host nobody
controls cannot be claimed. On your domain the login page carries your logo,
and only your company is reachable. Leave the field empty to go back to the
platform address.
