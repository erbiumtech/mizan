## What this is

Every interview scheduled, who is on the panel, what they said, and the
outcome. An interview is scheduled from the application — **Schedule
interview** — which also moves the application to the interview stage and
tells each interviewer.

## Feedback

Each interviewer has their own form: a rating, a recommendation, strengths
and concerns. **My feedback** opens yours; **Read feedback** shows the whole
panel's and names anybody who has not answered.

## Outcome <!-- requires: ApplicantUpdate -->

**Passed** leaves the application where it is, because the next round or the
offer is a decision somebody makes. **Failed** and **did not attend** reject
the application with that reason.
