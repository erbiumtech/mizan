## What this is

The year's holidays as twelve month grids. Public holidays are red and close
the company; optional and restricted ones are amber and close nothing. The
year buttons move back and forward.

## Keeping it up to date

Holidays marked recurring are offered for next year on the first of December
by the rollover; a moving date such as Eid still needs confirming. **CSV
Import → Holidays** brings a whole year in from a spreadsheet.
