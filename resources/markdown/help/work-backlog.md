## What this is

Everything open on a project that is not in a sprint, in the order you intend to do it. The order is
the point: a backlog is a ranked list, not a pile.

## Adding

The box at the bottom takes a title and a type, and Enter files it. Everything else takes a
default — the workflow's first status, the bottom of the list, nobody assigned.

That is deliberate. A backlog is written during a conversation, and a form that asks for an
estimate and an assignee before it will accept a bug is a form that gets skipped in favour of a chat
message. Open the item later for the rest.

## Ordering

Drag by the grip at the left. Dropping a row writes that one row — not every row beneath it — so
reordering a long backlog is instant.

## Into a sprint

Tick what you want and the buttons appear at the top: into any sprint that is not closed, or back
out of one. Planning is done in handfuls, so this is a bulk action rather than eight visits to eight
forms.

## The ↻ mark

An item that has been carried out of one or more sprints shows `↻` and a number. An item on its
third carry is one to talk about rather than roll again.

## Roles and permissions

Anybody who may create work items may add to the backlog. Reordering needs the same permission as
moving a card on the board.
