## What this shows

What the company owns, owes, and is worth, as at a single date — assets,
liabilities and equity. It's usually the first statement anyone outside the
company asks for, and the one the Trial Balance exists to prove is trustworthy
before you read it.

## Using it

Set **As of date** — it defaults to today — and the statement recalculates
for that date. **Download PDF** exports it.

## Roles and permissions

Requires `ReportView`, gated behind the Accounting module being enabled for
the company. Read-only — nothing here changes any figures.
