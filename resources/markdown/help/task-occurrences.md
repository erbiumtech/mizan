## What this is

Every instance of a recurring task that has come due: who it was for, when it
was due, and whether it was done, missed or excused. The nightly roll creates
them and marks the ones that lapsed.

## Excusing one <!-- requires: TaskOccurrenceUpdate -->

A missed occurrence that should not count — the person was on leave, the
system was down — is **excused** with a reason. The penalty is not applied and
the reason stays on the row.
