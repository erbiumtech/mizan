## What this is — Platform Administrator documentation

Every company's audit trail, in one list — the cross-company companion to
each company's own Activity Log page. A company administrator reading their
own trail keeps that ability on their own panel; what's added here is seeing
every company's at once, which is why a **Company** column and filter exist
on this version and not on theirs.

## Reading an entry

Same shape as a company's own Activity Log: **Model** affected, **Event**
(Created, Updated, Deleted), **Causer** ("system" for anything done outside a
logged-in request), and **When** — plus **Company**, to say whose trail this
row belongs to. Click a row to see the full field-level changes.

## Roles and permissions

Reachable only by a super admin. Like the company-level version, this is
read-only everywhere — nothing can be created, edited, or deleted through
either screen.
