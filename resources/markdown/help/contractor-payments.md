## What this shows

A year-end answer for people who are paid for work but aren't on the payroll:
what each contractor was paid, in total, for a chosen fiscal year. Payroll
answers this question for staff; nothing else in the app answered it for
contractors until this report.

## Using it

Pick a **Fiscal year** — it defaults to the current one — and the summary
updates for that year. There's no PDF export or filter beyond the year; this
is a single at-a-glance summary rather than a document meant to leave the app.

## Roles and permissions

Requires `ReportView`, gated behind the Accounting module being enabled for
the company. Read-only.
