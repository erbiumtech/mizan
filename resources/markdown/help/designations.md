## What this is

Designations as rows: a code, a level that orders the dropdown, and a pay
grade. The grade is a monthly-basic band; a salary package outside it is
warned about on the package form and never blocked, because a band is a
policy and a package is a decision.

## Adding one <!-- requires: EmployeeUpdate -->

Click **New**. Renaming follows the employees who hold it; one with people in
it cannot be deleted.
