## What this is

Where each field is printed on the leaf: the payee, the amount in words and in
figures, the date, and the crossing. Positions are in millimetres from the
top-left corner of the cheque. One layout per bank account, or a **Default**
with no account that every other account uses.

## Getting it right <!-- requires: ChequeUpdate -->

Click **Test print** and print the PDF at 100% scale onto a blank sheet. Lay
it over a real cheque against a window, read off how far each field is from
where it should be, and correct the numbers. Two rounds are usually enough.

The paper size is the leaf itself — measure it with a ruler — and the printer
must be told the same custom size, or it will centre the page on A4.
