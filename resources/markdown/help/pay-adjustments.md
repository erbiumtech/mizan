## What this is

A dated one-off line for one employee in one month: an Eid bonus, an extra
deduction, an advance recovered in cash. It is added to what the month would
otherwise pay on that line, and reaches the payslip the moment the month is
raised, or at once if the payslip already exists.

## Adding one <!-- requires: PayslipUpdate -->

Click **New**, pick the employee, the month and the line, and type the amount
and the reason. A negative amount takes money off. Once the month is signed
off the adjustment is frozen with it.

## Why not type it on the payslip?

A figure typed on a payslip has no date and no reason, and it is lost when
the payslip is recalculated. An adjustment is a record: it says who decided
what, and the payslip reads it every time.
