## What this is

Jobs you are hiring for.

## Openings and closing <!-- requires: VacancyView -->

**Openings** is how many people you need. The vacancy marks itself **filled** when the
last one is taken, so it does not sit open collecting applications for a job that no
longer exists.

**A vacancy anybody has applied for cannot be deleted.** Deleting it would take the
applications with it, and with them the record of who applied for what — which is the
memory this module exists to keep. Close it instead.

## The hiring manager <!-- requires: VacancyCreate -->

Optional, and absent entirely for a company that has not bought the Employees module — a
company hiring its very first person has no employee to name. The whole pipeline works
without it.

## Roles and permissions

**View**: `VacancyView`. **Create / update / delete**: `VacancyCreate`, `VacancyUpdate`,
`VacancyDelete`. The Employee role holds none of it.
