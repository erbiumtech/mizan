## Advance Schedule

Each advance in recovery and what it takes in each of the next twelve
months, skipped months stepped over, with the month's total across everyone.

## Advance Defaulters

Advances behind their schedule: what the instalments say should have come
back by now against what the recoveries recorded, and how far behind. A
paused advance is listed with its reason, because it falls behind by design.

## Advance Disbursements

What was advanced in the month, to whom, of which type and on what terms.
