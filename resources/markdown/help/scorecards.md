## What this is

The merit ledger, weighted by designation, with quality and volume shown beside the
net.

## Quality and volume are never folded together

Two people on the same net score can be opposite employees: one who does little,
carefully, and one who does a great deal badly. A single number hides exactly the
distinction this report exists to show.

**Quality** is daily reports and badges — what somebody was judged to have done well.
**Volume** is recurring tasks and late marks — how much, and how reliably.

## The weights

From the designation's scorecard profile. The ledger records what happened and what
each thing was worth; the profile says what it is worth *to this job*. A salesman's
week should count volume more heavily than a draughtsman's, and the ledger cannot know
that.

**Unweighted** in the Profile column means the designation has no profile, so
everything counts once. That is the ledger as it was before profiles existed, and the
tile counts those people because they are being judged on a ledger nobody weighted for
their job.

## The period

The fiscal year to date, ending on the date on the report. People with nothing on the
ledger in that period are left off.

## Roles and permissions

`ReportView` and the Performance module.
