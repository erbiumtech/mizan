## What this screen is

What you earned and what you spent over a tax year, broken down by category.
This is where "how much did I spend on education this year?" gets answered.

Where the **Balance Sheet** shows where you stand right now, this shows what
happened over a period.

## The tax year

Everything here is grouped by tax year, which runs **July to June** — the same
year Pakistan uses, so the totals line up with what a return would ask for.

A small naming trap worth knowing: FBR names that period after the year it
*ends* in, so July 2025 to June 2026 is FBR's **Tax Year 2026**. This app labels
the same period **2025-2026**. Same twelve months, two conventions.

Which year an entry falls into is decided by the entry's own date, not by when
you typed it in.

## How to read it

- **Income** — totalled per income category, so you can see what came from
  salary against what came from anything else.
- **Expenditure** — totalled per expense category: Education, Rent, Food,
  Transport, and whatever else you have set up.
- **The difference** — what you kept. Negative means you spent more than you
  earned that year, which is worth knowing even though nothing stops you.

## What is not counted

**Transfers between your own accounts.** Moving money from your bank to your
cash account is not income and not spending, so it appears in neither total.
This is why recording a cash withdrawal as a transfer rather than an expense
matters — otherwise the same money would look spent twice, once when you
withdrew it and again when you actually used it.

## Getting a useful breakdown

The categories on this screen are your own expense accounts, so the breakdown is
exactly as detailed as your chart of accounts. If everything lands in "Other
expenses", this screen cannot tell you much.

Splitting out the things you actually want to track — Education separately from
general spending, say — takes a moment on **My Accounts** and makes this screen
worth opening.

## Quick answers

**A category is missing.**
Categories with nothing recorded against them in that year are not listed.
Check the year selector first.

**The totals do not match my bank statement.**
This screen only knows what you have recorded. Anything you have not entered is
not here, and there is no automatic import from a bank.

**Can I see more than one year at once?**
Not on this screen — it reports one tax year at a time. Switch years to compare.

**My salary is not showing.**
Check the entry's date is inside the year you are looking at, and that you
recorded it as income rather than as a transfer into your bank account.
