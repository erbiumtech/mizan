## What this is

What each person did today, in their own words, filed at the end of the day.
A verdict — substantive, thin, or off topic — is proposed automatically and a
manager confirms or overrides it. The verdict is what earns or costs merit
points.

## Filing one

Click **File today's report** and write it. One per day; a day already filed
cannot be filed again.

## Deciding <!-- requires: DailyReportDecide -->

Open a report, read the proposed verdict and the questions it raised, and
decide. The decision writes the merit entry.
