## What this is

Cashing in leave outside a separation: a request for a number of days of an
encashable type, with the amount the company pays for them and the payslip
month it is paid with. Approving it debits the balance at once — as an
adjustment, so the balance says why it fell — and sends the amount to that
payslip as a bonus line.

## Deciding <!-- requires: LeaveRequestApprove -->

**Approve** checks the days are there to encash; **Refuse** needs a reason.
