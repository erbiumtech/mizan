## What this is

Who has a work anniversary in the next ninety days, in the order they fall.

Ninety days rather than this month, because the point of the report is to be read
*before* the day arrives. A list of anniversaries that happened last week is a list of
things nobody did.

## Years, counted properly

The years are **continuous service**, not the distance from the joining date on the
record. Somebody re-employed after a break has two spans and only the current one
counts — congratulating a person on ten years when six of them were somewhere else is
congratulating them on the wrong thing.

The same rule the turnover figures use, for the same reason.

## Milestones

The years a company usually marks — 1, 3, 5, 10, 15, 20, 25 and 30 — are flagged, and
counted on their own tile. Everything else is still listed, because a company that
marks every year can, but the tile is the number worth looking at.

Somebody whose anniversary falls inside the window but who has not yet completed a
year is left off: there is nothing to mark yet.

## 29 February

An anniversary on the 29th falls on the 28th in a common year. That is what every
company does and what the report does.

## Roles and permissions

`ReportView`, like every report, and the Employees module.
