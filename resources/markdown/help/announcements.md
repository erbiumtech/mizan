## What this is

Notices to the whole company or to a department, shown on the dashboard and
sent by e-mail to everyone they address. A closed factory, a new policy, a
holiday moved.

## Posting one <!-- requires: AnnouncementCreate -->

Click **New**, write the title and the body, choose who it is for and whether
it is pinned. It goes out when it is published, not when it is saved as a
draft.

## Aimed by rule

**By rule** sends the notice to whoever matches the conditions when it is
published — the night shift at one location, everyone under a particular
manager, staff in their first month. **Who matches today** shows the list
before publishing.
