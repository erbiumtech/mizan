## What this is

Notices to the whole company or to a department, shown on the dashboard and
sent by e-mail to everyone they address. A closed factory, a new policy, a
holiday moved.

## Posting one <!-- requires: AnnouncementCreate -->

Click **New**, write the title and the body, choose who it is for and whether
it is pinned. It goes out when it is published, not when it is saved as a
draft.

## Who it is for

**Everyone**, **one department**, **one location**, **everybody with one role**, **one
person**, or **by rule**.

A role audience reads the roles on somebody's account, so "everybody who is Manager"
reaches whoever holds that role in this company today — not a list somebody has to
keep up to date.

A person audience is one employee. It exists because the alternative people reach for
is an email, and then there is no record that the thing was said.

## Category and priority

**Category** is this company's own filing — Policy, Office, Holiday, whatever you keep
under Company Settings → Dropdown Options. It is shown on the announcement and
filterable.

**Priority** is three options and will not become more. A priority list long enough to
need thinking about is one where everything ends up "urgent", which is the same as
having none.

## Publishing later

Leave **Publish at** blank to publish by hand. Put a date and time in it and the
announcement publishes then: it appears on the dashboard that day, and the audience is
told that morning by the scheduled run.

One thing to know: the telling happens on the first scheduled run after the date. If
the scheduler is down for two days, anything whose date fell in that gap is on the
dashboard but nobody was emailed about it.

## Archiving

**Archive** takes an announcement off this list and off the dashboard, and deletes
nothing. It is different from expiring: expiring hides it from the dashboard on a date
you set in advance, archiving puts it away now.

"When did we tell everybody about the new leave policy" is a question somebody asks a
year later, and the archive is where the answer lives. Archived rows are hidden by
default and one filter click away.

## Aimed by rule

**By rule** sends the notice to whoever matches the conditions when it is
published — the night shift at one location, everyone under a particular
manager, staff in their first month. **Who matches today** shows the list
before publishing.
