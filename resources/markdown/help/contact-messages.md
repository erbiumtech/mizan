## What this is — Platform Administrator documentation

What visitors sent through the public contact form. Every active super admin
is notified when one arrives; this page is the inbox they share.

## Reading and answering

Nothing is typed here. Open a message to read it; reply from your own mailbox
to the address it carries — the form does not send mail on your behalf, so a
reply is a real conversation rather than a template.

**Mark handled** records who dealt with it and when, and takes it off the
default view, which shows new messages only. Switch the status filter to see
everything.

A message that is spam, or a duplicate, can be deleted once dealt with.

## What the form keeps out

The form refuses silently: a submission that fills the hidden field, or more
than a few from one address in a minute, is answered with the same thank-you
page and stored nowhere. If a visitor says they wrote and nothing is here,
that is the first thing to ask about.

## Roles and permissions

Reachable only by a super admin.
