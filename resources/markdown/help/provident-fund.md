## What this is

Each employee's provident fund as a ledger: opening balance, the monthly
contributions, profit credited, withdrawals. Append-only — a mistake is an
adjustment entry, never an edit — the same shape as the merit ledger, for
money.

## Where entries come from

Monthly contributions arrive on their own when a payroll run is signed off,
at the rates under the statutory settings, one entry per payslip. Opening
balances, profit and withdrawals are recorded here by hand
<!-- requires: PayslipUpdate -->.

## The settlement

A final settlement reads the fund's balance to the leaving date and pays it
out as its own line.
