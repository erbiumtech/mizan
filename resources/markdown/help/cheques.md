## What this is

Every cheque ever printed, including the voided ones. A cheque is printed from
a payment — **Payments → Print cheque** — not from here.

## Statuses

- **Printed** — out of the book, not yet seen on a bank statement.
- **Cleared** — the bank statement line matched the payment; the payment is
  paid.
- **Void** — spoiled, lost or cancelled. The reason is kept, and the payment
  went back to the pool to be paid again.

## Void and reprint <!-- requires: ChequeUpdate -->

**Void** needs a reason and sends the payment back to approved. **Reprint**
voids this number and prints the next one for the same payment in one step.
The number is never reused.
