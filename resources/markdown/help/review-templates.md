## What this is

What a review asks about: competencies, each with a weight, adding up to 100.
The template marked **Use for new cycles** is put on every review when a cycle
is opened, and each competency becomes a line the employee and the reviewer
rate.

## Weights <!-- requires: ReviewUpdate -->

A template is used only once its weights add up to 100 — otherwise a weighted
rating would mean nothing. The form says what they currently add up to.

## The scale

Ratings run from 1 to the company's scale, under Company Settings. The
weighted average of the rated competencies becomes the self and manager
ratings on the review, rounded to that scale.
