## What this is

A contract as a record — type, number, dates, gross, probation — rendered
from the company letterhead with a verification QR, and with a life: draft,
active, ended. Activating one ends any earlier active contract for the same
person.

## Accepting <!-- requires: EmployeeDocumentView -->

The employee named sees the contract and **accepts** it in the panel. The
time and the network address are recorded, as a payslip acknowledgement is;
the page says so and asks for that disclosure. Witnesses are optional. The
**signed scan** is attached as an employee document, so a dated contract
reaches the expiring-documents report.
