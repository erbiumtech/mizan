## What this is

Your half of a performance review: you rate yourself on each competency, say why, add your
comments on the period, and submit. Your reviewer then sees your ratings beside theirs.

It appears here only while a review cycle is open, asks for a self-review, and your reviewer has
not yet submitted theirs. Nothing waiting means there is nothing to do.

## Rating yourself

Each row is one competency from your company's review template, with its weight. **Rate** gives
it a score on the company's scale and a short note: what you did that shows it. The weighted
average of your scores becomes your self-rating on the review.

A review without competencies asks instead for one overall rating, under **Comments**.

## Submitting

**Submit my self-review** hands it over. Every competency needs a rating first. Your reviewer is
told, and from then on your ratings and comments are fixed — they are your account of the period,
and they stay as you wrote them beside the manager's.

## What you do not see here

Your manager's ratings. A review is a draft about you until your manager shares it; when they do,
it appears under Reviews.
