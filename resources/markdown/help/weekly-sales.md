## What this is

Visits, calls, quotes and orders per rep for one week.

## Where the figures come from

**If this company has the CRM module**, they are read from the pipeline: visits are
check-outs that week, calls are logged activities, orders are opportunities won
inside the week and their value. Nobody types anything, and the figures are a
consequence of working the week rather than a memory of it.

**If it does not**, they come from the Weekly Sales Form each rep fills in.

Which one is used is a licensing fact, not a setting, and the Source column on every
row says which you are reading. Where both exist the pipeline wins: a figure derived
from records that exist is not improved by one somebody remembered.

## Everybody is on the list

Including the quiet weeks. A sales report that dropped the people who did nothing
would be a report of the people who did something, which is a different and much less
useful document — "nothing recorded" against a name is the thing a sales manager is
looking for.

## The week

Monday to Sunday, taken from whatever date the report is run on. One value, so two
people cannot disagree about which week they mean.

## Roles and permissions

`ReportView` and the CRM module.
