## What this is

The company's departments as rows: a code, a parent for a hierarchy, and a
head. The employee record still carries the department's name, and the
dropdown every screen offers is kept in step, so renaming one here renames it
on every employee who holds it.

## Adding one <!-- requires: EmployeeUpdate -->

Click **New**. A department with people in it cannot be deleted; switch it
off instead and it leaves the dropdown while the history keeps its name.
