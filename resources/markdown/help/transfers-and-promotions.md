## What this is

Dated moves from the job history: a transfer changes the department, the
location or the manager; a promotion changes the designation. Both are
recorded from the employee's page with an effective date and a reason, and
the record's current values follow from the latest row.

## Rehiring

A leaver's page offers **Rehire**: the record reopens as a new spell with a
new joining date, and the old spell stays in the history.
