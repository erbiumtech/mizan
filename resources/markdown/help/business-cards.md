## What this is

Business card orders, and the queue they wait in: requested → approved → printed →
collected.

## Asking for cards

**Ask for cards** needs a quantity and nothing else. Anybody may ask — there is no
permission for it, the same way there is none for asking for a letter.

**Title on the card** and **Phone on the card** are overrides. Leave them blank and
the card says what the employee record says, which is the point of this existing at
all: forty people's cards then carry the company name spelt one way, and the one
person whose title is different is different because somebody typed it here.

## The queue <!-- requires: EmployeeUpdate -->

One button moves an order along: Approve, then Mark printed, then Mark collected.
**Refuse** needs a reason, and the reason is shown on the order.

**Proof** downloads the artwork for one order so you can check it before anything goes
to a printer. Select several and **Artwork for these** puts them on one sheet.

## What is on a card

Name, title, the company, its address, the phone and email, and the website — all from
the record and the letterhead. The colours and typeface come from the brand kit under
Company Settings.

There is no QR. A business card is not a document anybody verifies; it is a thing
handed over in a meeting, and a QR on one is a QR nobody scans.

## Roles and permissions

Everybody can ask and can see their own orders. `EmployeeView` shows everybody's, and
`EmployeeUpdate` is what moves one along or refuses it.
