## What this is

Your own password change screen, reachable from the user menu by anyone
signed in.

**Name** and **Company Email** are shown but can't be edited here — for an
employee they're editable through their Employee record instead, where a
self-edit becomes a pending change request that needs approval. Letting this
page write them directly would skip that approval step.

To change your password, enter your **current password**, then the **new
password** twice to confirm it.

## Roles and permissions

No permission gate — every signed-in user, including Employees, can reach
this page and change their own password.
