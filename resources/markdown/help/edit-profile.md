## What this is

Your own password change screen, reachable from the user menu by anyone
signed in.

**Name** and **Company Email** are shown but can't be edited here — for an
employee they're editable through their Employee record instead, where a
self-edit becomes a pending change request that needs approval. Letting this
page write them directly would skip that approval step.

**Language** is yours rather than the company's: an office can run in English
while one person reads Urdu. Leave it unset to follow the company.

To change your password, enter your **current password**, then the **new
password** twice to confirm it.

## Confirming your phone and address

Where the company asks its staff to confirm those, **Confirm your details** above is
where you do it, and where you come back to after changing a number. The page is not
in the sidebar on purpose: most people finish with it once.

## Emails you don't want

**Emails to stop sending me** lists the kinds of email the application sends —
payslips, leave, approvals waiting on you, documents, invoices, scheduled
reports, monitoring alerts, announcements. Tick a kind to stop receiving it by
email.

Two things worth knowing:

- Anything you stop here **still appears in the bell** at the top of the panel.
  Stopping an email reduces what lands in your inbox, not what you can see.
- Emails about your **account and your access** cannot be stopped — an
  invitation, a verification link, a notice that access has been suspended.
  Those are how you keep or regain your account, and somebody who had silenced
  them would have no way back in.

The choice follows you across every company you belong to, and saves as soon as
you tick it — you don't need your password for it.

## Roles and permissions

No permission gate — every signed-in user, including Employees, can reach
this page and change their own password.
