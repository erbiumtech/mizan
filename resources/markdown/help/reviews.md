## What this is

One person's review in one cycle.

## A rating does not change pay <!-- requires: ReviewView -->

**Nothing in this module writes to a salary.** This is the most important thing on the
screen and the list says it next to every rating.

A rating is an opinion; a package is a versioned salary setting somebody approves. Wiring
the first to the second would make the appraisal a payroll instruction — and the first
disagreement about a rating would become a payroll incident.

**Suggested increment** shows a figure worked out from a configurable scale. It is a
suggestion for you to consider. If you agree with it, you save a new salary setting
yourself, deliberately, as you would any other change to somebody's pay.

## Submitted is not shared <!-- requires: ReviewUpdate -->

**Submit** records that the review is written. **Share with them** is what makes it
visible to the employee.

They are separate on purpose: a written review is a draft about somebody until a manager
decides to share it, and showing it earlier would make honest drafting impossible. Until
it is shared, the employee cannot see it at all — not even in a list.

## Roles and permissions

**View**: `ReviewView` — your own reviews once shared, and your reports'. **Create /
update**: `ReviewCreate`, `ReviewUpdate`.
