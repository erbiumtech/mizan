## What this is

What somebody is working towards, and whether they got there.

## Nothing computes these <!-- requires: ReviewView -->

**Metric**, **target** and **actual** are words and numbers you write. This application
does not measure anybody's goals for them, and a system that claimed to would be
measuring whatever was easiest to count rather than what mattered.

**Weight** is optional, for companies that weigh goals against each other.

## Cycles are optional <!-- requires: ReviewCreate -->

A goal can belong to a review cycle, or to none. Some goals outlive the cycle they were
set in, and some are set outside any cycle at all.

## Roles and permissions

**View**: `ReviewView` — your own and your reports'. **Create / update / delete**:
`ReviewCreate`, `ReviewUpdate`, `ReviewDelete`.
