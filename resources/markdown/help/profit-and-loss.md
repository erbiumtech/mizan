## What this shows

Income and expenses over a period, and the profit or loss they net out to.
Unlike the Balance Sheet or Trial Balance, this one covers a *range* of time
rather than a single date, because profit is something that happens between
two points, not at one of them.

## Using it

Set **From** and **To** — **To** defaults to today. Leave **From** empty and
the report covers everything up to **To**. **Download PDF** exports the
selected period.

## Roles and permissions

Requires `ReportView`, gated behind the Accounting module being enabled for
the company. Read-only.
