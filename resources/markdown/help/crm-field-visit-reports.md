## Visits per Rep

Completed visits this month per rep, with how many the person visited
confirmed. The confirmed share is the number a sales manager reads: a rep
whose visits are rarely confirmed is a rep to ask.

## Unconfirmed Visits

Every completed visit the person visited has not confirmed, oldest first,
with where the link went. A visit with nowhere to send the link needs the
contact's email or WhatsApp number filled in; one sent and unanswered may
want a resend from the visits list.
