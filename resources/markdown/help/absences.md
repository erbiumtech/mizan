## What this is

Who was absent this month, and whether anybody knew.

## Absent is not on leave

They are separate columns, deliberately. A day of approved leave is a day the company
agreed to; an absent day is one it did not. Adding them together is how an absence
report stops being read — the number is always large and never means anything.

Leave is shown beside the absence for context and nothing more. It is not added to the
tiles.

## Days nobody marked

The third column, and often the most useful one. An unmarked day is a day that **pays
as present** — nobody said otherwise — so a month with forty of them has no absence
figure worth trusting.

Saying so is more use than a confident zero. If this column is large, the answer is
not to read the first one more carefully; it is to mark the month.

## The order

Most absent first, then most unmarked. Somebody with nothing in either column is left
off the list entirely.

## Roles and permissions

`ReportView` and the Attendance module.
