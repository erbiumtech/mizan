## What this is

The numbered ranges of cheque leaves the bank gave you, one book per bank
account. Printing a cheque takes the next number from the active book for the
account it is drawn on; when the book runs out, the next one takes over.

## Adding one <!-- requires: ChequeCreate -->

Click **New**, pick the **Bank account**, and enter the **First number** and
**Last number** printed on the leaves. A **Prefix** is only for leaves that
carry letters before the number.

If some leaves were already written by hand, open the book and set **Next to
print** to the first unused one.

## Numbers are never reused

A voided cheque burns its number. The book moves on, and the voided row stays
in **Cheques** with its reason. That is what the bank expects to see.
