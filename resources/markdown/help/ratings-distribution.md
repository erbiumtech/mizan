## What this is

How the final ratings fell, per review cycle: how many people at each point of the
scale, what share that is, and the same broken down by department.

The **shape** is the point. A cycle where four-fifths of the company scored the same
number has not measured anything, and a cycle with nobody at either end has managers
who do not use the ends. Neither is visible in a list of individual reviews.

## Shared ratings only

A rating a manager has entered but not yet shared is still being argued about.
Counting it would make the distribution move under the people calibrating it, which is
the one moment this report is most used.

So a review counts here once it is shared, and keeps counting once it is acknowledged.

## The department rows

Indented under each cycle, with the average rather than a distribution: a department
of five people has no distribution worth drawing.

Together with the cycle rows they answer the question a calibration meeting actually
asks — *is this a company-wide curve, or one manager's*.

## The bars

Characters, not a chart. They are proportional to the largest band in that cycle, so
the longest bar is always full width and the others are read against it. It is there
so the shape is visible to somebody who is not reading the numbers.

## The scale

From Company Settings → *Rating scale*. Change it and this report changes with it;
ratings already given keep the number they were given.

## Roles and permissions

`ReportView` and the Performance module.
