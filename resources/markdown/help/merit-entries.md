## What this is

The merit ledger: every point credited or debited to an employee, with what it
was for. A late mark, a missed task, a substantive daily report, a badge. It
is append-only — a mistake is reversed with a new entry, never edited away.

## Reading it

The balance per employee is the sum of the entries. The **Merit Ledger**
report shows it per period; the review form shows it per person.
