## What this is

Credited against taken, per person per leave type, for the leave years covering the
date on the report.

It is the people question. *Leave Liability* answers the money one — what unused
encashable leave would cost if everybody took it. This one answers who is using their
leave and who is not.

## Both ends of "not" matter

**Low** is the one most companies miss. Somebody at ten per cent in September has a
year's holiday waiting to be taken in December, and that is a staffing problem in
December rather than a saving.

**Over a hundred** is somebody on a negative balance. That is a payroll problem now,
not a staffing one later.

The list is ordered lowest first, because the low end is the one nobody notices on
their own.

## Reading a cell

Each leave-type column reads **taken / credited** in days. Credited is everything in
the balance before anything was taken: opening, carried in, accrued so far, and any
adjustment somebody made.

A dash means **no leave year has been generated** for that person and type. That is
not zero days — it is a question nobody has answered. Open the year under Leave
Balances, or use the run button there.

## Why the figures match the employee's own screen

They are the same arithmetic, from the same service. Credited, taken and remaining
mean exactly what they mean on somebody's own balance, which is the only way a figure
here can be argued with.

Pending requests are **not** deducted. A balance that moved when somebody merely asked
would show the same last day as gone to two different people.

## Roles and permissions

`ReportView` and the Leave module.
