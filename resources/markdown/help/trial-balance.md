## What this shows

Every account and its balance, as at a single date, with total debits and
total credits proven equal. It doesn't say what the business is worth or how
it performed — it says the books add up, which is what the Balance Sheet and
Profit & Loss both quietly assume.

## Using it

Set **As of date** — it defaults to today — and the list recalculates for
that date. **Download PDF** exports it.

## Roles and permissions

Requires `ReportView`, gated behind the Accounting module being enabled for
the company. Read-only.
