## What this is

A list of the laws this software does something about, and what it does.

## What it is not

**It is not a statement that your company complies with anything.** Compliance is a
statement about your affairs — your contracts, your filings, your records, your
figures — and no software can make it on your behalf.

Every row is written in the indicative about the software: *holds the FBR salaried
slabs per fiscal year and withholds against them*. None of them says *complies with*.
That distinction is deliberate and the page says so at the top, because somebody will
quote this in a tender.

## Rates and thresholds are yours

Where the product holds a figure as data — tax slabs, leave entitlements, EOBI rates —
it holds whatever was last entered. They change with each Finance Act and each
provincial amendment, and keeping them current is the company's job. The screen that
holds each one is named on the row.

A slab table that was right in 2024 will quietly produce 2024's tax in 2027. Nothing
warns about that, because nothing here knows what the law says today.

## Modules you do not have

A law covered by a module this company has not licensed is still listed, marked with
the module it needs. That is the honest answer: the mechanism exists and you do not
have it, which is different from it not existing.

## Roles and permissions

No permission — anybody signed in can read it.
