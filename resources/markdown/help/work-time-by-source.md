## What this shows

For every person on every project, over the period: hours logged **here** with *Log time*, hours
synced in **from a tracker** such as Clockify, hours written by **smart commits**, and the number of
days on which the same person has both a logged hour and a synced one.

## Why it exists

Time can reach the timesheet through more than one door. A company that has chosen *Clockify* for
its stopwatch keeps *Log time* on items that have no Clockify task, and on every item for a person
whose Clockify account is not linked; a company that has chosen *either* has both doors open on
purpose.

Either way, a day on which one person used both is a day worth a look: the two entries may be the
same hour twice. **Days with both** is that count, and this report is the reconciliation a manager
runs. It is the reason *either* is allowed at all.

## What is counted

Only time on work items, and only the three sources above. An ordinary timesheet entry with no
work item and no source is not here; it belongs to the timesheet reports.

Hours are the timesheet's minutes, divided by sixty. Rates are not on this report, and the rate a
tracker carries is never read anywhere: the rate is this application's.

## Roles and permissions

Reading this needs **ReportView** and both the Work and Timesheets modules. Nothing on it can be
changed from here.
