## What this is

Recognition that is awarded, not earned automatically: Employee of the Month,
Five Years, Zero Late Marks. Each badge has a name, an icon and the points it
carries; awarding one to an employee writes a merit entry.

## Creating one <!-- requires: BadgeCreate -->

Click **New**, name it, choose an icon and set the points. Award it from the
employee's page.

## Earned by rule

A badge can award itself. Under **Earned by rule**, add a condition — a field,
how it compares, and a value — and as many as the badge needs: everything in
a group must hold, and any group is enough. *Who they are* comes from the
employee record (department, designation, location, years of service); *what
happened* is counted over a window (this month, last month, last three
months, year to date, all time). **Who matches today** lists the people the
rule selects right now, before it awards anything.

Each morning the rule runs and the badge goes, once, to everyone it matches
who does not already hold it. Three badges arrive with rules already written,
as examples to copy.
