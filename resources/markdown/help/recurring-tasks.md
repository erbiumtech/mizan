## What this is

Work that comes round on a cadence — a daily report, a weekly stock count, a
monthly return — assigned to a person, a designation or a department. Each
cadence produces an occurrence with a due date, and a missed occurrence can
carry a penalty in merit points or in pay.

## Setting one up <!-- requires: TaskCreate -->

Click **New**. Choose the **kind**, the **cadence**, who it is assigned to,
and what happens when it is missed. Occurrences are rolled out by the nightly
schedule and appear under **Task Occurrences**.

## Assigned by rule

**By rule** assigns the task to whoever matches the conditions on each roll:
a department and a designation, everyone at a location with under a year's
service, anyone whose missed tasks last month were above two. **Who matches
today** shows the list before the task is saved. A new hire the rule matches
has the task tomorrow; nobody has to remember to add them.
