## What this is

The company's own list of what people claim for — travel, meals, phone,
stationery — each mapped to the transaction type it posts as. The ledger does
not move: a category is a name on a line, and the type behind it is where the
money lands.

## Adding one <!-- requires: ExpenseClaimApprove -->

Click **New**, name it, and pick what it posts as. **Needs a receipt** is a
reminder on the claim form, not a block.
