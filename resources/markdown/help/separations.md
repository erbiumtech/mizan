## What this is

Leaving as a record: the type, the notice date and the last working day, with
a **clearance list** built from the records — kit still in hand, an advance
outstanding, a provident-fund balance — and the exit checklist's items. Each
is cleared or waived with a reason. The final settlement reads the list and
says whether the person is clear.

## What happens when

The record keeps the last working day on the employee at once. The day after
it, the status is set from the type and the record is closed. The login stays
open for the grace period under Company Settings → Lifecycle, then closes.
