## What this shows

A project's epics and releases on a calendar, month by month, with its sprints as a faint band
behind them.

- An **epic** is a bar from the earliest any of its work started (or it was created) to the latest
  anything under it is due. The filled part of the bar is how much of it is done, by count of
  children. Its dated children sit as ticks beneath it; a filled tick is finished.
- A **release** is a marker on the day it is planned for, or — once it has gone out — the day it
  did.
- A **sprint** is the band it ran, or is planned to run, for.

An epic with nothing dated — no due date on it or any child — is not drawn, because there is
nothing to draw it between. Give it or a child a due date and it appears.

## Read-only, on purpose

Nothing here can be dragged. The dates a bar is drawn from are the item's own due date and its
children's; change them there and the bar follows. Dragging a bar to change a date is a nicety on
top of a date field, and the field is one click away on the key.

There are no dependency lines between bars and no critical path. A blocked item says so on the
board and on its own page; a Gantt chart's arrows are a different product.

## Roles and permissions

Everybody who may read work items may read this. There is nothing on it to change.
