## What this is — Platform Administrator documentation

The hand-written pages of the public site: terms, privacy, about, and anything
else a visitor should be able to read without signing in. Everything else on
the site — pricing, the feature pages, the contact form — is generated from the
product itself and is not edited here.

## Writing one

Click **New**. The **title** is the heading; the **address** is the end of the
page's URL and is made from the title until you type one yourself. Letters,
numbers and dashes only, and no two pages may share one.

The **body** is Markdown: headings, lists, links and emphasis. HTML typed into
it is stripped on the way out, so a page cannot carry a script.

**Search description** is the one sentence search engines show under the
title. Left blank, the first paragraph is used.

## Publishing

A page is a draft until **Published** is on. A draft is invisible to the
public — it answers not-found, not forbidden — and is left out of the sitemap.
The first time a page is published its publication date is stamped.

**Order in lists** decides where the page sits in the footer and the sitemap;
ties fall back to the title.

## Roles and permissions

Reachable only by a super admin: the site belongs to the operator, not to any
one company.
